Your journey, in phases

You don't get random workouts. You climb in planned phases.

Each phase builds on the one before — about 6 to 8 weeks each — so progress compounds instead of stalling.

Phase 1

Foundation

≈ 6–8 weeks

Build the base most people skip. We lock in form, consistency and the daily habits everything else stands on.

  • Movement & technique that keep you injury-free
  • A nutrition rhythm you can actually live with
  • Training that becomes non-negotiable
Phase 2

Strength

≈ 6–8 weeks

Now we add load. Progressive overload and real structure turn that base into capability — your engine for the goal phase.

  • Get measurably stronger, week on week
  • Heavier work, more structure, smarter recovery
  • The muscle & work capacity your goal needs
Phase 3

Your Goal Phase

≈ 6–8 weeks

Built around your goal: recomposition, a shred, or hypertrophy. This is where the visible change lands — on a base that holds.

  • Recomp, shred or hypertrophy — your call with your coach
  • The transformation people actually notice
  • Results you keep, because the foundation is real

Quick answers

The questions we hear most

How much does it cost?

Online coaching is the core of every plan, and in-person sessions are priced as add-ons. We go through the exact investment on the call once we know what you need. There are flexible payment options.

Can I just book PT sessions?

Not on their own. Your plan, tracking and coaching all live in the online program, so every client is on it. Sessions are added on top if you need them.

Is it just a PDF plan?

No. Your nutrition plan lives in our own Transformation Portal, with live macros, swaps, logging and a food journal. Workouts are in Everfit. Gym, home or both.

How fast will I see results?

Everyone is different, so we never promise a number. Energy and sleep often improve first; measurements take longer. That's why we track trends, phase by phase.

I have an injury or a health condition.

Tell us on the call. Everyone completes a health check, and some people need their doctor's OK first. We work alongside your doctor, never instead.

I've trained with S2S Fit before. Can I use the voucher?

No, sorry. The voucher is only for new members joining S2S Fit for the first time. It isn't available to current clients or to anyone who has been enrolled with us before. If you're coming back, we'd still love to talk: book the call and we'll go through your options.

Can my partner join the call?

Yes, and they're welcome to join the program too. You each get your own plan and keep each other accountable.
